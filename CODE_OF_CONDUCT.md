# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We welcome respectful, specific, and technically honest participation. Be considerate, accept constructive review, focus criticism on the work, and do not harass or discriminate against others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and knowingly unsafe or misleading claims about analyzer coverage.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact options. The maintainer may edit, reject, or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.
